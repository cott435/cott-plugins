# 06 — the cap and `Mode: defer`

Phase 6. Bounds the after-build loop. When a round 2 or later closes `new kinds`, the row is
BLOCKED with evidence `profile r<n> new kinds (cap), open <heading>` while that round's
spec-change entry is open, and the driver asks: *one more round* or *defer*. *One more round*
grants the designer its run. *Defer* runs the profiler with `Mode: defer`, which appends the
open kinds to `docs/followups.md`, resolves its own spec-change entry and appends a `deferred`
round line, after which the section is DONE. It closes the gap phase 5 leaves: a section whose
rounds keep finding kinds would otherwise loop without the user.

## Decisions

- **The evidence carries the open headings**, after the design's string:
  `profile r<n> new kinds (cap), open <heading>; <heading>`. The driver needs them for the
  designer's `Spec-change:` field on *one more round*, and reads nothing but `status.py`.
- **`status.py --profile <pkg>/<section> --defer` prints the defer block.** The flag is explicit
  so that `--step PROBE` typed by hand at the cap never defers by itself: without `--defer` the
  block is the next profile run, as everywhere else.
- **The cap needs the entry to be the profiler's**: an open `spec-change:design` whose
  `Raised by:` starts `profiler`. A designer's or tester's own spec-change at round 2 is not a
  cap.
- **Decisions come first.** A new `repair` or `drop` kind's stub blocks the row under the
  existing first test of rule 1; the cap is tested after it, so the user answers the treatment
  before being asked about the round.
- **`run-package --defer` answers a profile cap *defer***, as it does a review cap and the paths
  cap.
- **A deferred kind is one line in `docs/followups.md`**:
  `- <pkg>/<section> — K<n> <name>: <count> of <denominator>, proposed <treatment> — deferred at profile round <n> (docs/sources/<token>.md)`.

## Specification

### `status.py`

- Rule 1 of the docstring gains: "or a `stage:` source's newest round line, at round 2 or
  later, ends `new kinds: …` and a `spec-change:design` entry raised by the profiler is open".
- **Evidence** gains item 8:

  > 8. **profile cap** — `profile r<n> new kinds (cap), open <heading>; <heading>`: every open
  >    `spec-change:design` ledger heading of the section.

- The `--profile` paragraph: **Mode** gains "`defer` with `--defer`"; and the sentence
  "`--defer` prints, per `stage:` source whose newest round line ends `new kinds: …`, the block
  with `Mode: defer`, that line's round and its commit; `--defer` without `--profile` prints
  `--defer needs --profile <pkg>/<section>` and exits 2."
- `section_state`, rule 1, after the gate-record test and before `spec = live_spec_changes(…)`
  is used for rule 2: for each `stage:` source, when the newest round line's round ≥ 2 and its
  verdict starts `new kinds:` and any open entry of kind `spec-change:design` has a
  `Raised by` starting `profiler`, return `BLOCKED` with the evidence above.
- `spawn_fields`: `mode:` is `delta` when the evidence holds `open ` and `spec-change:design`
  anywhere, not only at its start.

### `agents/profiler.md`

- Description gains a last-but-one sentence: "Mode defer - moves the kinds still open at the
  round cap to the backlog and closes the round."
- **Inputs** item 1, **Mode** — `profile`, `verify` or `defer`.
- **Hard rules** write list gains `docs/followups.md` on a defer run.
- `### Mode: defer`, after `### Mode: verify`:
  1. Read the profile; the open kinds are the ones the newest `new kinds:` round line names.
  2. Append one line per open kind to `docs/followups.md` (create it with the line
     `# Follow-ups` when absent), in the form under **Decisions**.
  3. In the section's ledger, on each open `spec-change:design` entry whose **Raised by** is
     the profiler's, set `Status: resolved` and `Resolved by: profiler — <Run:>` with the Edit
     tool, one line each.
  4. Append the round line `Round <n> — <date> — commit <Commit:> — deferred`.
  5. Commit the profile, the ledger and `docs/followups.md`; return.

done). "entry" is a `deviation` step: kind `spec-change:design`, `Raised by: profiler —
run-package data`; the `deviation` macro gains `raised_by` and `status` options if it lacks
them.
