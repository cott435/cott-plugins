# 08 — reviewer-architect

Phase 8. The reviewer gets the E1 rule for designer-raised contract-row deviations, its
`Commit:` from `status.py --rounds` (F12), its report path under the section, the closed return
form (E2), the shell-paths rule (E5), the E12 sentence on counts, and the gate record's new
line kinds. The architect writes change files under the affected package — one per package
for a repo-level change — sweeps answered `spec-change:design|test` entries to `resolved` at
the package close (W2), and moves every path. The gap it closes: four parallel `a` reviewers
in the audit resolved the same §10 entries four ways, and every one of them wrote `Commit:
HEAD`, a sibling's commit.

## Decisions

- **On a designer-raised contract-row entry that fails the E1 test, the reviewer sets it
  `rejected` and appends a `spec-change:contract` entry** with the same Clause and Said, the
  designer's Did as Found, and verdict `spec-change`. Reason: the design says which the
  reviewer may approve and routes the rest to `spec-change:contract`; two entries record both
  readings, and `status.py` re-opens PLAN.
- **The reviewer runs `status.py --rounds <pkg>/<section>` once, always**, for its `commit:`
  line; `Round:` still comes from the prompt when present. Reason: the design (F12) — "the
  reviewer already runs `--rounds` once and runs no other command"; a new spawn field would
  change the driver's block, which **What must not break** freezes.
- **The architect runs `sync_decisions.py --all` before appending a stub**, as its one
  permitted repo-writing command beside `git`. Reason: it is the one agent that writes
  `docs/decisions.md` directly, and an inbox stub left unmerged by a hookless session would
  otherwise get the same number.
- **W2 sweep**: at the close, an `open` `spec-change:design` or `:test` entry that
  `status.py <pkg>` no longer lists under `open spec-change` is set `resolved`, `Resolved by:
  sync-plan — <Run:>`. Reason: the design's W2 owners.
- **A rejected designer entry needs no CRITICAL line**: the appended `spec-change:contract`
  entry and the `spec-change` verdict are the record; an approved E1 entry keeps `Resolved by:
  —` (`sync-plan` fills it), as for any approved entry. Reason: the closed CRITICAL list.
- **The closed return form**: `Result:` and `Verdict:` stay the first two lines, then the
  lines listed; `CRITICAL:` is one line per finding, or one line `CRITICAL: none`;
  `Convergence:` appears on round 2 and later `full` runs and never on a `defer` run. Reason:
  the eval writer found each form question unsaid.
- **The W2 sweep is per kind**: an entry of kind `spec-change:design` (or `:test`) is swept
  when its section's row lists no open spec-change of that kind — a section with a live entry
  of that kind is at DESIGN or TEST, never DONE, and its entries of that kind stay. When
  `sync-plan` runs by hand with a section not DONE, it proceeds for what verifies and lists the
  section in its return. Reason: `status.py`'s column lists kinds per section, not entries.
- **Slug collision for per-package change files** is checked per directory
  (`docs/packages/<pkg>/changes/`); a repo-level change uses one slug across packages by
  construction. Reason: the design's "same slug".

"(and its entries in the older `docs/deviations/<pkg>/<section>.md` and `docs/deviations.md`)",
and "an open `docs/packages/<pkg>/changes/<slug>.md` (or a pre-2.2 `docs/changes/<slug>.md`)".

**Bash usage**, second paragraph:

> Bash is for `git diff`, `git log`, `git show` and `git blame`; for
> `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/status/scripts/status.py --rounds <pkg>/<section>`,
> run once: its `commit:` line is your report's `Commit:` — the section's last commit, never
> `HEAD`, which in a parallel batch is a sibling's report commit (F12) — and its `next round:`
> line is your round only when your prompt has no `Round:` line; and for `git add` and `git
> commit` of the files you wrote (**Commit**). Nothing else: no `cat`, `ls`, `grep` or `sed`
> in Bash (the Read, Glob and Grep tools do that), no edits by shell, …

**The evidence**: the `ELSEWHERE` bullet becomes "checks whose every located failure lies
outside this section's paths in files the run did not touch — a sibling's half-built module,
another section's red intent tests. Quote each under **WARNING** with the paths it names, as a
problem for that section, never as this one's." New bullet: "**`SKIPPED` lines** are
`repo`-scope pytest rows the gate leaves to CI. Informational: quote none, fail on none." The
header sentence: "a header naming the section and the attempt".

**Deviations**, replacing the first `proposed` bullet:

> - A **`proposed`** entry raised by the implementer or `pair`: set its **Status** to `approved`
>   when its **Why** holds and its **Clause** is internal to the section (a design item, not a
>   boundary shape, a public name or a contract row); otherwise `rejected`, a CRITICAL naming
>   it, **Resolved by** your report path.
> - A **`proposed`** entry with `Raised by: designer` whose **Clause** is a contract row or
>   heading — the designer's §10 items — is judged by the E1 test, the same test the designer
>   applied: additive and compatible — a new optional parameter, a helper the row does not
>   name, a step split in two, a new name nothing consumes yet — is `approved` (`sync-plan`
>   folds it into the contract at the close); anything a consumer must change for — a changed
>   signature, a renamed or removed public name, a changed shape or nullability — is
>   `rejected` with **Resolved by** your report, *and* you append a `spec-change:contract`
>   entry with the same **Clause** and **Said**, the designer's **Did** as **Found**, `Raised
>   by: reviewer — <Run:>`; your verdict is `spec-change`. Never approve a contract-row entry
>   outside that test, whatever its **Why**: three such approvals in the audit changed public
>   shapes other packages consume.

Every ledger path in the section is the new one; an appended entry's heading is
`## <pkg>/<section> — <date> — spec-change:<level> — <k>`.

**Report**: "Write to `docs/packages/<pkg>/reviews/<section>/<date>-r<n>-<letter>.md`, creating

**Commit**: the report path; "`docs/packages/<pkg>/deviations/<section>.md` or

**Return message**, replacing "Then, under 30 lines in all: …":

> Then, under 30 lines in all, exactly these lines and no other — the form is closed, and a
> fact with no line here is in the report, never in the return (E2):
>
> ```
> Result: done | blocked
> Verdict: approve | request changes | spec-change
> Report: <path>
> Counts: <k> critical, <w> warning, <s> suggestion
> Round: <n>
> Convergence: <k> prior unfixed, <m> new        (round 2 and later; else omitted)

>
> Every count is counted from the report you wrote — `<w>` is the number of bullets under
> **WARNING** — not remembered (E12).
