## Iteration 2 — the one fix

- `agents/designer.md`, template item 11: choose the `D<n> binds` form by what the decision is
  about, not by its `Scope:` — a question naming a call, setting or name another section owns
  is the second form, naming that section; the third form only for this section's own items
  nothing in the design depends on.
- `agents/designer.md`, Procedure step 8 (option 2a): every name the contract's **Section
  interfaces** gives the section has its own §5 row carrying the contract's signature and
  error cases, not a helper's row in its place.
- `agents/architect.md`, **Commit**: `git status --short` runs after the last edit and before
  the first `git add`, and not `git diff`, which lists no new file such as an archive copy.
