## What was tested

That the edits made for `2026-10-06-audit-run-package-96a768ee.md` keep every covered eval passing and close the audit's defects where an eval can see them:

- **Implementer:** the return is an exact-label template, `README: none` after a stop, and marker lines come from the grep re-run after the last write (E1, E3).
- **Tester:**
  - the stage check is scoped as `git status --short -- <paths>` (E2);
  - a decision binding this section gets no `Not written:` line;
  - a forbidden §7 case is found at step 1 (E7).
- **Designer:** one entry per wrong clause, headings one per line (U03).
- **Researcher:** a count in the return is the number, and redirect targets are literal paths (U15).
- **Driver:** a grant stays owed until its reviewer returns, an unanswered question is asked again, and `stopped because` is never the driver's own (E5, E6).
- **Write guard:** a Write that drops lines from an existing agent `MEMORY.md` is refused (E7).

## Method

- **Mechanical:** `contract_sweep.py` passed 54/54 before and after the evals. `build_site.py` wrote 118 pages (9 agents, 15 workflow skills, 22 knowledge pages), the same counts as before.
- **Write guard:** 8 direct stdin probes of `hooks/guard_writes.py` against a scratch repo with `docs/architecture.md`. No model was involved.
- **Behavioral:** `run-evals`, one run per configuration, working tree (`with_skill`) against a `dev-team-v2.8.0` snapshot (`old_skill`). There were 14 executor runs and 14 graders, all general-purpose subagents on Sonnet 5.5, graded with skill-creator's `grader.md`. No run errored or was rerun. Iteration 1 found a regression of this change (below), which was fixed, and iteration 2 re-ran implementer 13 and 18.
- **Set change, in this change:** implementer eval 18 gained one expectation pinning the return template. Iteration 1 showed that both graders passed a baseline return with `Path of the section README: not written (…)` and a trailing summary paragraph, so no expectation saw the U23/U53 defect.

## Verdict

The claims the evals can see hold, and nothing covered regressed: the working tree is at or above 2.8.0 on every eval. One regression of this change was found and fixed. The template said "comma-separated" for `Deviations:`, against the `; ` the set and the driver's **Regenerate** field use, and iteration 2 confirmed the fix.

**Not proven by a model run:**
- E5, the driver's grant after its implementer returns: no eval drives a granted cap round past its implementer.
- E6, an interrupted question.
- The tester's scoped `git status`.
- The researcher's return count and literal redirect paths.
- The designer's several-entry spec-change.
- The implementer's `Dependencies committed in <sha>` body line.

Each needs a new eval, with a fixture, before it counts as more than a stated rule. The run-package `writes.txt` / `reads.txt` expectations need the stream-capturing harness that `run-package`'s set was written for. Without it they fail for both configurations, so they say nothing here.
