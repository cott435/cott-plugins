## What was tested

That the 2.6 designer writes the `surface` design right after PLAN from the contract alone
(eval 11), writes a fenced skeleton ending `frames to effect: <n>` for each entry point a
**Call paths** entry names (eval 12), and raises `spec-change:contract` with the **Call paths**
line as `Clause:` for a frame **Section interfaces** does not define (eval 13), without
regressing the 2.5 surface-pipeline eval (eval 9); that the tester builds the early surface's
fakes to the contract's **Section interfaces** when `Dependency READMEs: none` (tester 9); and
that `run-package` spawns the surface designer beside `ingest` right after PLAN with
`Dependency READMEs: none` copied from `--fields` (run-package 15). Plus the two mechanical
rows: the new forbid claim and the state cases.

## Method

Mechanical rows: shell runs. 4.1: `contract_sweep.py`; then `designed last` planted in
`agents/architect.md`'s surface paragraph (`…a section like any other, but it is designed
last: …`), the sweep rerun, the file restored from a scratchpad copy and the sweep rerun.
4.2: `evals/fixtures/state-cases/check.py`.

Behavioral rows: `run-evals`' executor loop, one run per configuration, `with_skill` the working
tree and `old_skill` the `dev-team-v2.5.0` snapshot. Twelve general-purpose executors (proxy:
each is given the agent file as instructions, not the registered agent with its `tools:` and
`skills:`), one grader per run, `aggregate_benchmark`, and for tester 9 (100% vs 90%, within
ten points) one blind comparator. The twelve first runs were spawned in the background; their
completion notices went to the driving session, so `timing.json` exists only for the rerun of
designer 9 `old_skill` and the benchmark's time and token columns are empty for the rest.

Reruns: designer 9 `old_skill` was stopped by a safety classifier before the design was written
(an empty `outputs/docs/packages/data/design/` only), and rerun once on Sonnet: complete.
Designer 11 `with_skill` ended on an API safeguards error (Sonnet 5.5, `[reasoning_extraction]`)
with partial outputs and no transcript; the outputs were cleared and it was rerun once on Sonnet,
which ended on the same error (request `req_011CfeWALHWjtG9HkP6Scj53`). On the user's choice it
was then run on **Opus** (Agent tool `model: "opus"`), same prompt, cleared directory: complete.
Its with_skill result is therefore an Opus executor against a Sonnet `old_skill`. Designer 11 and
12 `old_skill` each had one reply cut off mid-run; both transcripts end on the full return and
were graded.

Set corrections (run-evals step 7, on the user's yes at review), then the affected runs were
regraded, both configurations, from the same outputs (no executor rerun: the outputs do not
change with an expectation):

- `run-package.json` eval 15 #6: forbade any Read of the contract past the Sections table, which
  a whole-file Read of the 23-line fixture cannot avoid and a prose transcript cannot show
  bounded; both configurations failed it identically. Now fails only on use of the **Call
  paths** or **Section interfaces** content.
- `designer.json` eval 12 #7 and #8: required the two error codes under Error handling and the
  Boundaries citation under Data model, placement clauses outside the skeleton rule; now
  anywhere in the design.
- `designer.json` eval 11 #1: its heading clause caught a `spec-change` heading in the old run's
  `interview.md`; now scoped to `outputs/docs/`.
- `tester.json` eval 9 #8: mapped an Interfaces row to `§5` only, while `tester.md` says `§<n>`
  is the design heading that states the item (`§3` for `DataError` and `DataSettings` here).
- `designer.json` eval 9: the parentheticals and note that named a 2.4.0 baseline now say a 2.5.0
  baseline passes (no verdict changes; not regraded).
