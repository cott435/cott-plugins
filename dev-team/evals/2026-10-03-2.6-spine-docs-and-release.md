found the cause and wrote it as its first WARNING. `_item(text, "Call paths")`
(`skills/status/scripts/status.py:419`) starts an item at any line that begins with
`**Call paths**`, numbered or not. The eval-16 seed's contract has such a line in **Section

not run against it.

## Iteration 3 — the fix

The user chose to widen the phase to fix the parser (option b of the review). One fix:
`_item` (`skills/status/scripts/status.py:419`) now starts a bold item only on a numbered line
(`\d+\.\s*\*\*<name>\*\*`). Every template that `_item` reads numbers its bold items:
`change.md` **Affected sections**, `repo-contract.md` **Toolchain**, `implementer.md`
**Entry points and interfaces** and **Public names**. Every unnumbered `**<name>**` at the
start of a line in the bundle is a prose wrap.
