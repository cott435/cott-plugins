## What was tested

The lint block caps positional parameters only (`PLR0917`, `max-positional-args = 5`) and
refuses a ruff older than 0.16.0 (V, from phase 0's PF-1). Both fixture suites pass on a ruff
at or above that floor, with two new cases pinning the cap and six cases repaired for ruff's
newer defaults. A new `forbid` claim keeps `PLR0913` and `max-args` out of the prompts and the
lint block.

## Method

- The shim `evals/workspace/bin/ruff` (gitignored, `exec uvx ruff@0.16.9 "$@"`, mode 755) put
  first on PATH for both suites: `PATH="$PWD/evals/workspace/bin:$PATH" python3
  evals/fixtures/<suite>/check.py`.
- Before any edit, under the shim: `hook-events` 177/183, the six failures exactly the six the
  note names; `state-cases` 114/114. As the note expected, so no deviation.
- The six failures had three causes, each found by running `uvx ruff@0.16.9 check --isolated` on
  the planted files:
  - `PLW0129` in `fmt-repo-config` (assert on a string literal).
  - `I001` in `gate-guarded-decision-constant`, `gate-report-fail` and
    `gate-xfail-wrapped-pass`. The fixture repo has no ruff `src`, so `data` sorts as third
    party, and the blank line between `import pytest` and `from data…` is now an import-block
    error.
  - `RUF100` in `gate-diff-before-review` and `gate-guarded-exception`, because
    `# noqa: I001` on a sorted block no longer suppresses anything.
- The repairs:
  - `assert "x…"` → `assert a == "x…"`. Still 103+ characters, so it is still the E501 line
    the repo's `line-length = 200` excuses.
  - The blank line between the two third-party imports is dropped.
  - The loader's block becomes `from pathlib import Path  # noqa: I001` / `import csv`. That
    is a real import-order finding suppressed by a `# noqa` at line 3, the line both cases
    assert on.
  - `gate-report-fail`'s format failure is deliberate (its `2 failures` are format plus intent)
    and was left in place.
- Row 1.3 was run in a scratch directory holding the lint block as `pyproject.toml` and the
  overview's `m.py`.
- Row 1.4 planted `PLANTED PLR0913 line` as line 173 of `skills/project-structure/SKILL.md`.
  The sweep ran, the plant was removed, and the sweep ran again.
