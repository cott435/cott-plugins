## What was tested

That the driver, rewritten by D1–D9, still walks one section to DONE (eval 1), routes a
designer `spec-change:contract` to the architect (2), asks a blocked decision once and prints
`uncommitted:` only when git says so (3), batches two independent sections together (5) and
one kind at a time under `--serial` (6); and that it now asks about a gate-BLOCKED row with
the record's lines and three options (7), hands two open `spec-change:design` headings to one
designer one per line (8), ends in the Summary on a free-text answer (9), and asks instead of
closing when every section is DONE and the surface check fails (10).
