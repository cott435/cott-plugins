## What was tested

Phase 6 (`site/notes/2.7-data-loop-06-cap-and-defer.md`): when a profile round 2 or later
closes `new kinds: …` while the profiler's `spec-change:design` entry is open, `status.py`
puts the row at BLOCKED with evidence `profile r<n> new kinds (cap), open <heading>`;
`--profile <pkg>/<section> --defer` prints the `Mode: defer` block; `run-package` asks *one
more round* or *defer* and on *defer* spawns the profiler with that block verbatim; the
profiler's `Mode: defer` moves the open kinds to `docs/followups.md`, resolves its own entry
and appends a `deferred` round line. Plus: nothing changes for a package with no `stage:`
source, the line budget holds, and the paths cap still asks as before.

## Method

Rows 6.1–6.4 are shell runs from `dev-team/`. Rows 6.5–6.7 are `run-evals` behavioral runs,
one run per configuration. Evals 18 and 13 run the driver in the executor loop against a copy
of the state cases `stage-cap` and `paths-cap`; the harness answers *defer* and records every
Agent call without sending it. Eval 7 is a proxy: a general-purpose subagent given
`agents/profiler.md` (working tree, or the copy at `b4fd022`) and the spawn block
`status.py --profile … --defer` would print, on the seed `evals/sets/files/profiler/build.py
case-7`; its `tools:` list and the write guard are not reproduced. Timing was recorded for
three of the six executors (the completion notices of the other three carried no usage); the
benchmarks' time and token rows for those runs read 0.
