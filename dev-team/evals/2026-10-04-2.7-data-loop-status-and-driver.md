## What was tested

That a section whose `source` cell is `stage:<token>` is PROBE until its profile's round 0
closes (no round line, `pending verify`, `revise: …`), that `status.py --profile` prints the
profiler's fifteen-field block, that a package with no `stage:` source prints byte for byte
what 2.6.0 prints, and that `run-package` sends that block verbatim to one
`dev-team:profiler` and counts it in the summary — without changing the PROBE, DESIGN and
paths-review spawns it already made.

  `--run-gate data`; `git diff --name-only dev-team-v2.6.0 -- evals/fixtures/state-cases`.
- **2.4** `git diff --numstat dev-team-v2.6.0 -- skills/run-package/SKILL.md` and

  be sent, and both configurations sent it into their own copy). One grader per run;
  `S finalize`, `aggregate_benchmark`; blind comparison on iteration 2 (both 100%).
