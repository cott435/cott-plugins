## What was tested

That a ledger entry whose heading ends `— <k>` stays open while its `Status:` reads `open`,
whatever is committed after it; that PLAN, DESIGN and TEST evidence names every open heading of
the level; that a probe doc re-opens a built design only when a line the design was written
against is gone or reworded; that the new contract claim catches a renamed evidence form; and
that the researcher, re-probing a source whose doc exists, keeps the shared body byte for byte
and adds its own entry (rows 3.4 and 3.5).
