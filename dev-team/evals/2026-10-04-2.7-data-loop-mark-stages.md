## Verdict

Rows 4.1–4.5 hold. Row 4.6 misses its second condition: eval 1's brief (`two-package`) has a
`clean` section that drops duplicate rows and sorts the export `ingest` reads, which is a
section "whose job is to clean … data its dependencies produce", so the new paragraph marks
it, as the design says it should. The note's premise that eval 1 is "a package contract with
no data-heavy section" does not hold for that fixture. No fix was made: narrowing the
paragraph so the two-package `clean` goes unmarked would contradict the design's rule. The
outcome is a Deviation in the phase note. The user chose to commit as is (option a): the
marking is correct per the design, and the bar's "no `stage:`" condition rested on a wrong
premise about the fixture. No `feedback.json` (static viewer; the reply came in chat).
