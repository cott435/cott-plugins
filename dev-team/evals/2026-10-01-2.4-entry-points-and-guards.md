## What was tested

That `entry_point.py` adds, extends and replaces one line under `[project.entry-points."<group>"]`
as a text edit that leaves the rest of the file byte for byte, reports an unchanged line, quotes a
dotted group and key, and refuses a target outside the package, a module with no file and the
`console_scripts` group; that the Bash guard lets `entry_point.py` through only under `locked.py
deps` for the caller's own package, acts from `docs/brief.md`, lets a researcher redirect outside
the repo and words a redirect refusal as `may not redirect to <target>: it is outside
.dev-team/tmp/`; that the write guard acts from `docs/brief.md`, refuses a `surface` implementer a
sibling section's code and refuses an added suppression comment under `tests/intent/`; and that
`hooks.json` changed only its `description`.
