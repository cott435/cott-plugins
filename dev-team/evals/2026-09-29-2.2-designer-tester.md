- **Graders:** one per run, skill-creator's `grader.md`, on Sonnet 5.5 (the user's instruction this phase; `site/notes/CLAUDE.md` updated to match).
  - The grader prompts were first staged inside each run directory, which executors may read. They were moved to the session scratchpad once that was noticed, after the first five had been graded.

- `aggregate_benchmark` wrote each iteration's `benchmark.json` and `benchmark.md`. Viewers were served on :3117 (designer) and :3118 (tester).
