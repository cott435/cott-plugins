## What was tested

That `status.py` makes the `surface` row ready at PLAN, DESIGN and TEST once the package
contract has a `## Call paths` heading, keeps it waiting for every other section from IMPLEMENT
on and at every state without the heading, and that `--fields` prints a seventh line,
`dependency readmes:`, listing only the dependency READMEs that exist on disk (`none` when none
does).

## Method

Shell runs from `dev-team/`, no model. 3.1: `python3 evals/fixtures/state-cases/check.py` on the
working tree; then the six new cases against the 2.5.0 script, a copy written with `git show
dev-team-v2.5.0:dev-team/skills/status/scripts/status.py` to the chat's scratchpad, by importing
`check.py` and setting its `STATUS` to the copy for that one run (no edit to `check.py`, so
nothing to revert). 3.2: `contract_sweep.py`. 3.3: `build.py fields-new <dir>`, then
`status.py --fields data/ingest` inside it.

## Verdict

The claim holds. The surface is ready right after PLAN only when the contract carries the
heading, its IMPLEMENT still waits, a contract without the heading keeps the 2.5 timing, and
`--fields` names only READMEs that exist. One departure from the note's code, recorded as a
Deviation in the note: `dependency_readmes` walks the Sections table rather than the `depends
on` cell, so the list is in the Sections order the note's decision and docstring promise for
every section, not only for `surface`. `build-site` wrote 104 pages (8 agents, 15 workflow
