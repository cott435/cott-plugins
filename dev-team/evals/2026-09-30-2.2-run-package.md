## What was tested

- **9.1** — the bundle's contracts after the driver and `pair` edits.
- **9.2** — regression: the driver still walks one section to DONE (eval 1), routes a designer `spec-change:contract` to the architect at PLAN (eval 2), and asks a BLOCKED decision once and records it (eval 3).
- **9.3** — the design's smallest end-to-end slice: with two independent sections `ingest` and `sessions` ready together, `/dev-team:run-package data` sends both implementers in one message, each gate record names its own section, and both sections reach REVIEW after that batch (eval 5).
- **9.4** — `--serial` restores the 2.1 loop: one kind per batch, the two implementers in separate batches (eval 6).

## Method

- **9.1** — `python3 ../plugin-dev/scripts/contract_sweep.py`, then the note's **Done when** greps.
- **9.2–9.4** — real headless driver sessions, as remake phase 8 ran this set: `dev-team:<agent>` in this session resolves to the installed plugin, so an executor subagent acting as the driver would spawn the installed agents.
  - **Setup.** A fresh `reset.sh` copy of `evals/fixtures/two-package/` per eval and configuration, seeded per the eval's harness, with a committed `.claude/settings.json` disabling the installed `dev-team@cott-plugins`.
  - **The session.** `claude -p "<the eval's command>" --plugin-dir <P> --model sonnet --output-format stream-json --verbose --permission-mode bypassPermissions --disallowedTools AskUserQuestion --append-system-prompt <H>`, run from the copy. `<P>` is a scratch copy of the working-tree plugin without `evals/` for `with_skill`, and the same with `skills/run-package/` replaced by the baseline snapshot's for `old_skill` (agents, hooks and `status.py` are the working tree's on both sides). `<H>` replaces `AskUserQuestion` with an append to `outputs/interview.md` answered from the harness's **Answers** (inlined), and for evals 5 and 6 inlines the harness's **Where the walk ends**.
  - **Outputs.** `spawns.md` (main-thread Agent calls; a batch is one assistant message id), `status-log.txt`, `transcript.md` and `summary.md` (the final `result` text) were derived mechanically from `stream.jsonl`; `git-log.txt`, `git-status.txt`, `status-final.txt` and `repo/` (with `.dev-team/gate` and `stop`) are read-only copies. Runner: `run.sh` and `post.py` in the session scratchpad.
  - **Scope.** The hooks fired: every session's cwd was the copy, so the gate, the write guard, the Bash guard and the sync hook ran for real.
  - **Order.** Eval 5 ran first, both sides together; the `with_skill` runs of 1, 2, 3 and 6 started once eval 5's implementer batch had gone out as specified.
  - **Cost.** with_skill $5.04 + $6.61 + $4.67 + $7.40 + $6.84; old_skill $6.09 + $7.01 + $5.01 + $8.09 + $0.85; about $58 in total. Wall time 953–1272 s per `with_skill` run.
- **Graders.** One per run, Claude Sonnet 5.5, the skill-creator grader, told how the outputs were derived and allowed to read `stream.jsonl`. Told also that an `outputs/interview.md` append is the harness's stand-in for the question: the first eval-3 grader read it as the driver inventing an answer.
- **Blind comparison.** Not run: the pass rates differ by 33 points and the note's rows do not say `blind`.

## Set corrections

Graders' critiques named three expectations that a run following the target cannot pass. Each was corrected in `evals/sets/run-package.json` under `note_2_2_phase9`, and the affected runs were regraded:

- **Eval 1 #2 and #6, eval 3 #1** required the designer, the first `status.py data` or the question before *any* spawn. The target's Loop step 1 spawns the `Scaffold: data` implementer before anything else, so these now allow it, as eval 5 #1 already did. Eval 1 old_skill went from 5/7 to 6/7; eval 3 old_skill from 4/6 to 6/6 (this correction and the grader's `interview.md` note).
- **Eval 3 #5** required `uncommitted: docs/decisions.md` even when an agent's commit had carried the driver's edit, a claim the outputs contradict. Two graders flagged it. It now requires the line to agree with `git-status.txt`. with_skill (no line, file committed by the implementer) went from 5/6 to 6/6; old_skill (line present, file committed) now fails it, 6/6 to 5/6.
