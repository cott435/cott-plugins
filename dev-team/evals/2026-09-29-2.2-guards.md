## What was tested

That the write guard confines an implementer spawned with `Section: <pkg>/<section>` to its section's files: code, unit tests, ledger, inbox, scratch and marker. On `surface` that adds `interface.md` and `mkdocs.yml`. It falls back to the role-wide rule for a `Scaffold:` run or a missing transcript, and every role's globs know the 2.2 paths. The new Bash guard refuses a dev-team agent's shell write to a repo file: a redirect outside `/dev/null` and `.dev-team/tmp/`, `sed -i`, `tee`, or `python -c` opening for write. It lets `locked.py` through, and fails open on a command it cannot parse.
