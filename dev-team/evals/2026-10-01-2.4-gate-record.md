## What was tested

That the stop gate writes a `commit:` line on line 2 of every record and records a marker stop
instead of deleting the only trace; that `status.py` derives BLOCKED from a current `blocked` or
let-through record and IMPLEMENT from a current `not done` record, ignores a stale, pre-2.4,
`report` or `spec-change` record, stops reading the record once a review round covers the code,
and prints the two-command `next:` line; that the new contract claim catches a renamed result
value; and that with no record present `status.py` prints what it printed at the plan commit.

## Verdict

Holds. The gate record names its commit, a marker stop leaves a record, and `status.py` holds a
blocked, let-through or died-between-attempts section on it while ignoring every record that
does not speak for the current commit. Suites: state cases 95/95, hook events 159/159,
contracts 44/44. No fix was needed.
