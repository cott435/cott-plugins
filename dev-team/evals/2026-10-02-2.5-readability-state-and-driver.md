## What was tested

Once every section of a package is DONE, `status.py` prints a `paths:` line, re-opens a
section a current paths report names below round 3 as `FIX n` with evidence `paths r<m>
request changes (<report>)`, hands the report to `--inputs`' `Review:` and a sixth `--fields`
line, and holds `shipped:` until the review approves (a package with no `[project.scripts]`
is `approved (no commands)`). The driver spawns the **Reviewer — PATHS** block on `paths:
needed`, runs a re-opened section like any FIX row, and asks *one more round* / *defer* at the
paths cap; the 2.4 driver evals (gate-BLOCKED question, two open spec-changes, a free-text
answer, a failing surface check) still hold.
