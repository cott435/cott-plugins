## What was tested

That `status.py --paths <pkg> --against-contract` prints the contract's **Call paths** entry
beside each command's tree (`match`, `extra`, `missing`, the effect `reached`, a `summary:`
line with the command's own budget) and refuses the flag without `--paths`; that the bundle's
contracts still hold; and that the paths reviewer reads that comparison: a six-column **Paths**
table whose `contract` cell is copied from the summary, an `extra` frame a CRITICAL `break`
under the section that owns it, P1 judged against the command's **Call paths** budget, and a
contract without the heading reviewed as 2.5 did.

## Method

- **5.1** `python3 evals/fixtures/state-cases/check.py`, all 171 cases (six new
  `calltree-contract-*`); `status.py --against-contract` and `--rounds x/y --against-contract`
  from the plugin directory. Also, by hand, the script on the eval fixtures (`paths/` +
  `paths-clean/` + `paths-contract-extra/` or `paths-contract-budget/`, copied to the scratchpad):
  its output equals the block the eval 12 and 13 prompts supply, except for the frame column's
  padding (the prompts write `1 cli.load  match`; the script pads to the longest frame).
- **5.2** `python3 ../plugin-dev/scripts/contract_sweep.py`.
- **5.3, 5.4** `run-evals` on `reviewer`, evals 12, 13, 10, one run per configuration, the
  working tree (`with_skill`) against the 2.5.0 snapshot (`old_skill`). Before iteration 1, eval
  10 expectation 5 was corrected: it named the five 2.5 columns, which the 2.6 template no
  longer writes; it now accepts the six columns with `contract` `—` or, from a reviewer before
  2.6, the five. Iteration 1: six executors in one batch; `old_skill` of evals 12 and 13 ended
  on a Sonnet 5.5 safeguard error (`[reasoning_extraction]`), their outputs were emptied and each
  was rerun once, successfully. Iteration 2, after one fix to `agents/reviewer.md`: the three
  `with_skill` runs anew; the three `old_skill` run directories (grading included) copied from
  iteration 1, since the baseline ref, the prompts and the fixtures are unchanged. Graders read
  skill-creator's `agents/grader.md`, one per run.
- After iteration 2, eval 12's break-line expectation was loosened on its grader's critique: the
  literal `… of `data-load` — ` continuation rejected the `(also P4)` form `reviewer.md`
  prescribes for a frame that is both. The run it was critiqued on passed it; no verdict changes,

`agents/reviewer.md` now says every `[unresolved]` leaf of a block is a WARNING or a
SUGGESTION, one bullet each with its `file:line` and the call, a WARNING when it hides where a
path goes and a SUGGESTION when it does not, never left out. Iteration 2 reported the leaf in
all three evals and passed 35/35. Grader notes not acted on: eval 13's grader liked the
reviewer's extra WARNING (the contract's three listed frames cannot fit its own budget of 1),
